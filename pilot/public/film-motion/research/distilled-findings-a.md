## Tools implied by visible relationships, without claiming actual tools

Claude’s icon graph can be built with SVG/DOM and deterministic timeline animation. Runinfra’s grids, masks, typed copy and charts suit SVG/DOM/GSAP. Notion’s linked mobile panels suit layered2D transforms, with depth added only where perspective materially helps. Frameboy needs genuine geometry for tactile closeups, device rotation and cartridges; retain screen content as an independently controllable surface. Leap’s repeated material geometry and highlights justify a3D scene, while its text and actual product UI should stay separately controllable. These are implementation proposals, not proof of source-code choices.
