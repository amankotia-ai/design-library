## Transfer proposals for software films

1. **Repeat with development.** Establish one routine task, then show the same semantic object in changed contexts. Keep data/state continuity; each repetition should add understanding rather than merely accelerate.
2. **Use a cumulative layout when accumulation is the point.** The center can recede during an intentional overload passage; it need not stay the brightest element. When the film reaches actual product proof, establish a readable destination. A second layout can close the argument with a changed result. Test subject identification rather than assuming a fixed central panel is clear.
3. **Alternate density and intimacy.** Combine quiet composition, precise detail and concentrated montage. Neither editorial nor high-motion direction requires constant movement.
4. **Motivate materials.** Paper, screen, archive, reflected image and physical action can represent different kinds of evidence. A SaaS film must use verified product states and appropriate brand assets rather than borrowing this film's personal memories or footage.
5. **Compose before adding finish.** Separate subject/background values and palette roles in layers. Treat selective focus and texture as attention tools; preserve sharp product proof and type where comprehension requires them.
