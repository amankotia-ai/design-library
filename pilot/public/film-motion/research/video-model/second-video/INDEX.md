A public audio stream and a low-resolution video stream were recovered. A complete local ASR pass produced 318 timestamped segments, and a 31-frame sampled contact sheet was inspected. ASR contains recognition errors, especially tool names; it is not human-verified speech or an audio-quality review. Sampled frames do not establish original-speed motion or complete visual coverage. Raw ASR ends 0.119 seconds beyond the measured media duration, reinforcing the need to validate timestamps before using them in an edit.

## Why the comparison does not calibrate a quality equation

The presenter describes supplying the same raw footage to both makers, then revising the automated draft with references and specific criticism. This motivates preserving inputs and correction history. It does not establish randomized viewer outcomes, equal labor accounting, matched accepted quality or a representative failure rate.

The spoken and on-screen time summaries differ and are approximate case reports. Do not divide them into a universal productivity multiplier or use displayed charges as current market prices. Record active labor, elapsed time, machine runtime and spend separately in our own tests. Include asset selection, prompting, retries, cleanup, export, review and collateral repairs.

Two independent experiments are useful next: change one meaningful picture/speech relationship and test comprehension; then benchmark one bounded revision request under matched assets and acceptance criteria. Faster iteration can help us explore better edits, but it receives no automatic artistic-quality points.
