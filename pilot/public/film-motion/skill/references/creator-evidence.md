This compact record accompanies [creator-craft.md](creator-craft.md). Full media, every-frame CSVs, sheets, hashes and review logs stay in the MovieMaker project's `research/creator-studies/`. The additional consecutive window f414–453 documents changing light states, rather than deriving them from one still. The film credits Gawx for direction/shooting/editing/co-writing and YouTube for production/writing/mix/color; authorship is distributed. No exact light or grading setup is inferred.

**[ROUTINE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GoTa5ETmQYw), 6 June 2023.** All 2,457 decoded frames received a consecutive 160px-thumbnail scan across 39 sheets, supplemented by 16 larger views, a 32-frame finish window and two working-source-size stills. Approximate cue bands:

A central portrait is an attention anchor, not independently validated product proof. An ending still alone cannot establish its hold duration. These personal materials teach structure and attention, not reusable brand assets or product claims.

## Schiffer: planning and a constructed transition

**[How I Plan Shots & Transitions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3mZuJAWH1Ow), 13 June 2020.** The 14,788-frame tutorial was completely indexed, then sampled every 24 frames: 617 inspected thumbnails / 25 sheets at 240px. A separate pass inspected 168 consecutive frames at 320px, f14280–14447 (595.000000–601.958333s). The passes overlap: 785 viewings / 778 unique source frames. This is not a full every-frame scan.

Visible lists around 89–91s, 120–122s and 167–170s distinguish entities, actions and ordering. The dense window shows distinct scene replacements: f14309 blade detail; f14334 held cup/ingredients; around f14350–14355 a tabletop edge rises as red blending imagery replaces the lower image and then fills the view. This supports an edge-mediated join, not an uninterrupted physical move or a verified software recipe. No spoken tutorial content or soundtrack quality was inferred.
