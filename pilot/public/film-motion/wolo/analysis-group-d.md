Art direction is nearly black with white sans-serif headings, orange-red brand accents, and green, magenta and purple data colors. Dense UI panels often dissolve into the black field instead of sitting inside decorative device mockups. Titles are substantially larger than UI labels and occupy deliberately vacant regions. Grayscale footage and subtle scanlines appear in the introduction; chart evidence uses a cleaner treatment. The faint whale supports one idea without becoming an all-purpose background decoration.

The principal motion is frontal reframing, crop travel, character reveals, local pointer action and data-state changes. There is no need to infer a complex 3D camera from the scaling of flat interface material. The sequence gives several seconds to meaningful inspection and form completion; copying only its fast headings would lose most of its explanatory value.

**Consecutive window:** [heatmap activation](windows/hyperdash-heatmap/index.html), f240–287 / 10.000–11.958s, 48 frames on two inspected sheets. The pointer reaches the toolbar; the framing pulls back; the purple layer builds across the chart. The wider composition is established as the new information arrives. This supports a staged transition, rather than an instantaneous whole-screen replacement.

Transferable decision: give a UI chapter five explicit states—label, target, input, visible change, readable result. Move the crop when the next piece of evidence requires it. Maintain full-context recognition between detail views.

## PVP × Hyperdash

Source: [full film](https://media.wolo.digital/work/pvp-hyperdash.mp4?v=9f923e28). Duration 45.760s; 1920×1080; 24fps; 1,098 decoded frames. [Timestamped overview](studies/pvp-hyperdash/index.html): 183 inspected thumbnails on sheets 00000–00007, from 0 to 45.5s.

| 38.5–45.76s | Short central typed claim, deletion/replacement into the Hyperdash wordmark, then the PVP relationship beneath it. | The montage's density collapses into a compact lockup held on a dark ground. |

Three art systems share this film: green-black cinematic scenes lit by practical-looking lights; charcoal product cards with mint, magenta and purple states; an abrasive editorial collage with serif category words, duplicated text, newspaper crops and electronic interference. The consistent dark value range and recurring orange caret connect these sections despite the material changes. The evidence does not establish how the character footage was made; it should not be labeled AI-generated or CG on appearance alone.

The opening builds suspense by revealing place, computer, character and action in sequence. The middle compresses interface content into large, isolated components. The second montage then accelerates visual change through hard replacements and disrupted text. This is a four-part construction—atmosphere, mechanism, escalation, identity—not a uniform high-speed treatment.

**Consecutive window:** [screen-to-product handoff](windows/pvp-screen-entry/index.html), f312–347 / 13.000–14.458s, 36 frames on two inspected sheets. The completed CRT title remains legible, a short bright flash crosses the cut, then clean product copy, the purple strip and chart elements settle in sequence. This is a flash-covered edit and reframing; a continuous geometric morph is not demonstrated.

Transferable decision: a cinematic opening earns its place when a visible object or action carries the viewer into the product. Change texture and intensity at chapter boundaries, while preserving a small brand cue. Protect the clean explanatory middle from the distortion used in the montage.
