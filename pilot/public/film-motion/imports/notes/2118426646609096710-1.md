**Transfer:** Show purposeful partial details before the full product reveal; carry action through a physical input (cartridge) to a changed screen state; stage diagram and live output side by side to explain a concept; make the ambient world respond to the demonstrated state. Macro, rotation, and pullback earn their place because they reveal material, object geometry, or input/output relationship. Depth and controlled defocus suit physical surfaces here; that does not imply oblique blur is suitable for text the viewer must read.
