On Unifize, the same decision is one record with one owner.

How it works.

Nobody stops to write it up. The record is made as each decision happens, so nothing is typed twice or rebuilt for an audit.

  1. 01

    Rules stay current.

    Every procedure, drawing and form has one version in force. When a revision is approved, the old one is superseded everywhere it is used, and the people who work to it are retrained.

    • The old revision is superseded the moment the new one takes effect
    • Training goes to the people the change touches
    • Documents can stay in SharePoint
  2. 02

    Decisions record themselves.

    Approvals, answers and evidence are captured where the work happens, in the record's thread and checklist. Nobody re-types a decision into a tracker or an email.

    • A name and a time on every step
    • Approvals signed under 21 CFR Part 11
    • Evidence attached to the record, not to an inbox
  3. 03

    The company learns.

    Because every decision is a record, the timings come from ordinary work: how long changes take, where they wait, and which fixes did not hold.

    • Closure time, month by month
    • Where work waits, and on whom
    • Fixes that did not hold, sent to their owner
