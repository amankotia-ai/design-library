The case for Plasticity
No other CAD software gives you this much power at this price.
From vehicle design to industrial design to video game art, Plasticity gives you the tools to create perfect solids and surfaces.
Boolean Precision
Execute complex Boolean operations with watertight results, even on dense or sculptural geometry.
Studio only
XNURBS Surface blending
Create ultra-smooth, G2-continuous surfaces between even the most irregular shapes. Achieve precise, seamless blends with minimal setup.
Fillets and Edge Control
Apply curvature-perfect fillets that adapt intelligently to edge conditions.
Dimensions and Precision
Add live dimensions that update geometry instantly. Model with engineering-grade accuracy while keeping the flexibility of direct-editing workflows.
Direct Editing
Push, pull, and adjust existing geometry without starting from scratch. Drive edits with dimensions and PMI. Modify CAD data instantly with no history tree required.
Why Plasticity
Plasticity is a powerful complement to other CAD tools. History-based parametric modeling is valuable but costly: to keep feature trees robust, many advanced solid and surfacing tools are disabled. Plasticity is free of these constraints and exposes the full power of Parasolid and xNURBS. With Plasticity, you can rapidly create precise surfaces and complex solids that would otherwise be blocked by laborious history workflows.

What are the system requirements for Plasticity?
Plasticity benefits from modern, high-performance hardware. While you can use the free trial to check how well it runs on your system, we recommend the following for a smooth experience:
CPU: Modern multi-core processor, such as Intel Core i7 or AMD Ryzen 7
GPU: Dedicated graphics card (e.g. NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660, RTX series)
RAM: Minimum 16 GB (32 GB recommended for large projects)
Plasticity can run on both laptops and desktops, but a powerful CPU, GPU, and sufficient RAM will greatly improve performance — especially when working with complex 3D models.
How does a node-locked, individual-use license work?
A node-locked license is tied to specific computers:
Indie license: You can use Plasticity on up to two of your computers at the same time.
Studio license: You can use Plasticity on up to four of your computers at the same time.
We provide:
10 activations per license (per one-year period). You can request additional activations at any time.
Licenses are strictly for individual use and cannot be shared between multiple people. Even if installed on multiple machines, each license may only be used by a single individual. Concurrent use by different people is not permitted.
What is a perpetual license?
Both our Indie and Studio licenses are perpetual, meaning you pay only once for a 12-month maintenance window, but you own and can use the software forever. With your purchase, you receive 12 months of free upgrades, encompassing releases within that period. The versions of the software released and installed within the 12-month period will not become invalid or inaccessible after one year.
We aim to have two or three major releases each year.
What operating systems does Plasticity run on?
Plasticity is available for:
Windows (10+)
macOS (12+)
Linux (Ubuntu 22+)
Please note that not all features are available on every platform.
Your license can be used across multiple devices and operating systems — you’re free to install Plasticity on Windows, macOS, and Linux simultaneously.
