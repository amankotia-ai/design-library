## Character to preserve

The page feels like a quiet production tool: near-black page, narrowly separated dark surfaces, thin low-contrast rules, near-white medium-weight sans headings and muted supporting prose. The serif wordmark and large serif testimonial are the deliberate contrast; do not turn every heading into serif or monospace. The exact public font files may be retained by implementation, with attribution and usage limits recorded separately. Source CSS is stronger evidence than visually inferred hex values.

At desktop, the hero uses two distinct reading planes: large two-line promise on the left, restrained right-aligned explanation and a small white CTA on the right. A broad issue-detail product window begins beneath them. The product's dense labels, status markers, avatars, code diff and activity timeline carry credibility. Preserve their native scale and crop, rather than replacing the demo with generic cards. The mobile reference stacks headline, copy and CTA at a 24px edge, then crops the intentionally wide window inside the page boundary. Do not shrink every product label to fit mobile.

The subtle red-to-violet-to-blue atmosphere exists in the source around key proof surfaces. It is subordinate to the product and has no role as an action affordance. Faithful reproduction should retain its source proportions; new library chrome should stay neutral. It must not become a broad new multicolor visual theme.
