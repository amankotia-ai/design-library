# Mockup Templates import

Template asset owner `/root/template_packs`. The run imports finished work from the sibling Render Assets project into the Mockup Templates collection. Each download is the original source ZIP. Each gallery image is a JPEG derivative of a named archive member, with the member’s original pixel dimensions recorded in the catalog.

| Pack | Gallery contents | Download contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand & packaging templates | 30 blank packaging, print, retail, device, and social scenes | Final PNGs, manifest, original notes, and packed Blender scenes |
| Oak retail scenes | 5 oak and limestone scenes | Final PNGs, artwork guides, packed Blender scenes, and source notes |
| Warm wood scenes | 4 sunlit compositions used for the ARCO examples | Final PNGs, references and attribution, notes, and packed Blender scenes |
| Mobile scene templates | 7 fixed-camera phone and campaign scenes | Original-size plates, masks, placement geometry, and packed Blender scenes |
| Dark wood phone | Blank phone plate and screen placement guide | Original-size plate, guide, mask, light map, reference, and Blender scene |

The archive approach completed all five packs after parallel iCloud hydration. The expanded pack contains thirty variable-size plates, with the artwork and lighting constraints stated in the original notes. Oak and warm wood use the fixed photographic framing and source artwork surfaces. Mobile and dark wood use the photographed hardware and protected screen masks. The dark wood guide shows the reference composition and placement boundary. It is distinct from the blank plate supplied for reuse.

Visual asset review covered the original four source overview sheets, the imported mobile and warm wood covers, a retail stationery plate, and both dark wood previews. The source ZIPs passed full member CRC checks. `template-entries.json` contains the gallery fragment. `template-provenance.json` records archive and original image hashes, dimensions, and cover members. Independent gallery review is recorded separately.
