# Film & Motion — practical learnings

The collection now leads with 16 practical lessons and nine motion labs. Research logs, chronological video breakdowns and verification reports are no longer collection cards. The 36 remaining production guides and team roles have their own compact text view.

Each lesson card names a concrete principle, explains its value and gives a use case. Its detail page includes three application steps, an authored before/after example and a specific caution. References are secondary and link back to the existing source notes; those records are preserved rather than deleted.

The lessons are synthesized from the existing supplied research. They do not claim a new viewing of the source videos. Administrative records and repeated archive documents are not mechanically recast as creative advice. The content module records source entry IDs and exact section headings so the reasoning can be traced.
