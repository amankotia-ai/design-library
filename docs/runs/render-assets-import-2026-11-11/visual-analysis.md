## Gallery direction

Prefer a faithful full-frame image preview using `object-fit: contain`; these compositions deliberately use varied crops and empty space. Avoid arbitrary portrait crops that hide device geometry or turn a multi-shot contact sheet into unreadable fragments. Family cards may use a representative final image and provide the contact sheet in the detail view. Keep shared library typography, fine rules and restrained controls; let the render imagery carry color.
