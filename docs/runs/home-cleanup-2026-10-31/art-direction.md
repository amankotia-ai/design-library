# Homepage cleanup art direction

Reviewer: /root/cleanup_art_direction. Inspected `before-desktop.png` visually and read the actual `system-browser.jsx` / `system-browser.css`, task brief, and visual/product analysis. This pass defines direction before implementation; it is not approval of a delivered revision.

The page's useful visual identity is already carried by its mark, quiet neutral frame, three real previews, restrained cards, and collection names. Remove the photographed explanatory blocks, intro slogan and paragraph, numeric preview badges, card-signature slogans, duplicate count banner, and homepage footer copy. Leave all preview imagery intact: the lettering within those samples describes the work being browsed.

Retain the existing 1136px desktop content span inside the 1184px wrapper, 24px card gaps, border/radius tokens, 1.55 preview aspect ratio, and single-column mobile cards. Keep the small catalog count on the same row as the plain “Design systems” H1 at desktop, stacked at mobile. Preserve family, study/expansion mode, palette/type labels, section counts, and destination actions.

Use the existing catalog heading's 18px/500 treatment for its new H1; a new hero-sized title would recreate the removed framing. Change card titles to H2 beneath that H1, keeping their existing 23px visual treatment. Retain the 20px catalog-heading-to-grid gap and 20px card-body inset. After removing signatures, use about 16px between the collection title and palette row; do not reserve the old 44px signature height. The existing palette-to-divider gap is sufficient.

Tighten homepage header-to-catalog spacing from 76px to roughly 48px desktop and 32px mobile. Leave header brand/navigation dimensions and top spacing intact. End the catalog with 48–64px bottom padding, without a replacement decorative footer or separator. Do not change other routes' layout or study/source content.

Review criteria: images become the first substantial content after the compact heading; cards stay equal-height at desktop; there are no empty remnants of removed blocks, new ornaments, or clipping at 390px/768px/1440px; navigation and Explore links remain intact and visibly focusable.
