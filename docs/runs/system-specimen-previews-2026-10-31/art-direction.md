## Shared composition

Use one static DOM specimen board per system in the existing 9/10 footprint. Preserve the outer card, caption, palette circles, typography metadata, and name link. Compose a prominent type sample in the top half, a registered three-color strip below it, and representative component forms in the lower third. The board should read as a compact identity sheet with three scales of evidence, rather than a site with a header, hero and follow-on sections.

Small section labels such as Typography, Palette, and Elements are useful. Keep them subordinate. Make the typographic sample, large color areas, and shape contrast legible without reading microtext. No new decorative animation, gradients or shadows.

## Quiet Serif board

Paper ground `#f8f8fa`, ink `#202020`, thin rule `#dedee0`. Use the local Editorial/Noto Serif Display face at weight 200 and native 62.5% stretch for a dominant two-line sample such as the registered “Strong form. / Quiet direction.”; a short General Sans alphabet or numerals supplies the secondary type voice. Align this sample to the left and leave an open right-hand margin.

Use three adjoining palette rectangles, then an asymmetric lower composition: a small sharp photographic crop on one side and a fine-rule editorial link/caption treatment on the other. A plain text-arrow treatment is more faithful to this language than pill controls. Keep photography a modest material sample, not a large hero split. Retain the existing asset provenance and substitute-font disclosure internally.

## Blueprint board

Use blue `#104bff`, paper `#f7f7f8`, ink `#18181b`. A large Geist `Aa` or brief registered type sample is the anchor; a small Geist Mono number line establishes the technical secondary voice. Show blue as a substantial flat surface, paired with a pale rounded sample panel. Avoid reproducing the Blueprint landing-page headline/layout.

The lower panel can show one rounded action-shaped span and a compact parameter-style row, both static. A blue action on paper and dark text fields carry the editor's shape language. Do not introduce the yellow generated artwork as a system color. The color strip uses only registered swatches.

## Delivery criteria

At 1280/1440px, both boards should show their type, palette and element treatment clearly. At 768px, protect the dominant type and one element before microcopy. At 390px, a one-column board should preserve the same hierarchy without horizontal overflow. Demo elements remain noninteractive; only the card's system navigation performs an action. Page cards retain their live page viewport.

These are original system overview compositions. This art-direction pass does not claim delivered-output QA or source-layout recovery.
